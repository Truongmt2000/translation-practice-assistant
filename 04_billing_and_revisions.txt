Translation Practice Handbook
Section: Billing and revisions
Source: practice_handbook.pdf, page 4

Invoices are issued after the client confirms receipt of the final files. The invoice lists job name, word count, rate, and any rush fee.

One round of small revisions is included if the request arrives within 10 days and does not add new source text. New source text is a new job and needs a new quote.

Unbilled work is tracked in the job log with the date, client name, and reason it was not invoiced yet. Unbilled work is reviewed every Friday.

Payment terms are net 15 days unless the client contract says otherwise.
