Translation Practice Handbook
Section: Quality and delivery
Source: practice_handbook.pdf, page 3

Every delivery includes a spelling pass and a meaning pass. The spelling pass checks names, numbers, and dates against the source. The meaning pass checks that the tone matches the original (formal notice vs marketing).

Deliveries are sent as the same file type the client sent, unless the client asked for a different format in the quote.

If a source sentence is unclear, the translator adds a short translator note instead of guessing. Guessing is not allowed for prices, dates, or legal terms.

Late delivery must be flagged as soon as the delay is known, not after the deadline has passed.
