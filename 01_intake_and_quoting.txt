Translation Practice Handbook
Section: Intake and quoting
Source: practice_handbook.pdf, page 1

New translation jobs start with a written request from the client. The request should name the source language, the target language, the deadline, and the file type.

A quote is based on word count, subject difficulty, and turnaround time. Marketing copy and legal or technical text are quoted at a higher rate than simple notices.

Rush jobs with a deadline under 24 hours must be confirmed in writing before work begins.

The client must approve the quote before translation starts. Work that begins without approval is not billed as a rush job unless the client later agrees in writing.
