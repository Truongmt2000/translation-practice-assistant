Translation Practice Handbook
Section: Files and confidentiality
Source: practice_handbook.pdf, page 2

Client files are stored only in the approved project folder for that job. Files are not copied to personal USB drives or public cloud links.

Customer names, account numbers, and draft translations are confidential. They must not be shared in email threads with people who are not on the job.

When a job is complete, working copies are kept for 90 days in case the client asks for a revision. After 90 days, extra copies are deleted from local folders.

If a file looks like it contains payment cards or passwords, stop and ask the client to send a redacted version.
