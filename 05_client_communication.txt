Translation Practice Handbook
Section: Client communication
Source: practice_handbook.pdf, page 5

Status updates are sent when a job is accepted, when translation starts, and when files are delivered. Silence for more than two business days during an active job is not allowed.

Clients may ask questions in email. Answers about policy must match this handbook. Do not invent extra discounts or extra revision rounds.

If the client asks for work this handbook does not cover, for example on-site interpreting or video subtitles, say that this practice does not offer that service.

Office hours for client messages are 9 AM to 5 PM Pacific Time, Monday to Friday, excluding statutory holidays.
